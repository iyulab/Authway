Authway

Modern OAuth 2.0 / OpenID Connect authentication system built on Ory Hydra.

Features

  • OAuth 2.0 / OIDCStandards-compliant authentication with PKCE support
  • Multi-TenancyFully isolated tenant support
  • MFA (TOTP)Encrypted secrets and backup codes
  • PasswordlessMagic-link email and social sign-in
  • WebhooksSubscribe to account and session lifecycle events
  • TypeScript SDKs@authway/client and @authway/react
